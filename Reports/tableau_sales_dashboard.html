Tableau Sales and Profitability Dashboard Review

Cameron Batts

Problem

Present regional sales, category trends and customer profitability with a clear distinction between source-data calculations and dashboard previews.

Dashboard design and review

Review the static dashboard exports and independently aggregate the included Orders worksheet by region and calendar year. Check row IDs, date coverage, distinct orders and customers before comparing headline figures.

Findings

The included Orders sheet contains 10,194 line items dated January 3, 2022–December 30, 2025, with 804 distinct customers and 5,111 distinct orders. Unfiltered totals are $2,326,534.35 in sales and $292,296.81 in profit (12.56% profit/sales). West leads regional sales at $739,813.61, followed by East at $691,828.17. Technology totals $272,419.17 for calendar year 2025; that figure is annual, not quarterly.

Limitations

The executive preview and the included Orders spreadsheet use separate sources: the executive package embeds Sample - Superstore, while the sales package embeds Orders + Manager. The executive preview displays 2,703 customers, 6,455 orders, $8.952M sales and a 15% profit ratio; those exact KPI values and filter scope have not been independently recomputed from its extract. The map value near $457,688 is a state-level figure, not the West regional total. Packaged workbook definitions were inspected, and the sales workbook's basic rendering and interactions were subsequently checked manually by its owner. Executive-workbook interactions, data refresh and embedded Hyper queries remain untested. Dataset provenance is not fully documented; filenames containing 2026 do not imply 2026 sales.

Data and provenance

The Excel file contains Orders, Managers and Inventory worksheets. A separate traffic-accident CSV is bundled but is not used for the sales summaries. Source files remain unchanged.

Verification

Analysis/verify_sales.py recomputed the included spreadsheet totals. Analysis/inspect_workbooks.py checked the two supplied Public packages, their XML definitions, formulas and declared actions. ZIP integrity checks passed for all four uploaded variants. Tableau was not opened during the automated review. The owner subsequently reported successful manual loading and interaction checks for the sales workbook. The Hyper query attempt was blocked by the local environment before any extract data was read.

Owner-reported Tableau checks

On October 22, 2026, Cameron reported that Sales-Customer-Analytics.twbx opened normally in Tableau, filters updated charts and totals, clearing a filter restored the original view, tooltips displayed correctly, and chart selections behaved as expected. These are owner-reported manual checks, not independently observed runtime tests. Executive-Sales-Metrics.twbx, data refresh, embedded-extract queries and reconciliation of displayed KPI totals remain unverified.

Reading the dashboard

The dashboard exports combine maps, annual category trends, customer tables and an executive KPI example. Read each export in its own filter and data context; use the source summaries for independently reproduced totals.

Packaged workbook review

The supplied executive and sales packages are now available. Their source definitions explain the different data scopes. ZIP and XML checks confirm two executive dashboards with nine declared actions, and a sales dashboard plus story with fourteen worksheets.

The executive profit ratio and sales aggregate margin both divide summed profit by summed sales. The sales Shipping Target parameter defaults to three days and evaluates average shipping days at the current view grain. The executive workbook also contains a relative-date calculation using TODAY(); reopening can shift its displayed dates without adding new sales.
